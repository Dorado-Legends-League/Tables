Dorado Legends League league table

Classic scoring: every manager's starting eleven scores FPL Draft points each gameweek and the totals decide the table. "Since the league began" is the official table from Gameweek 4, the first week the league scores. "Whole season" shows where everyone would stand had the league counted from Gameweek 1: each manager's best legal eleven in Gameweeks 1 to 3, since no lineups were set before the league started, then the eleven he actually started from Gameweek 4, using real FPL points. Injury Time is the green row. Click a team for its week-by-week points.
