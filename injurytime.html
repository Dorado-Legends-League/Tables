Injury Time Statistics

"Since the league began" counts only weeks a player was in your starting eleven, from Gameweek 4, as the league table scores him; "Whole season" adds Gameweeks 1 to 3. Bubbles are his last three games, oldest left, then the period total: 1 green beats the position average for a game, 0 red falls short, grey is level. DNP: did not play for you (no minutes or on your bench; hover to see which and the points unused). SOON: game not kicked off yet. Hover a bubble for the game and the average. # is his points rank at his position this season. Red row: out or 75% or less to play, suspended, or left the league. Yellow row: worth trading or releasing; hover the name for the reason and the target. Yellow line under a name: match in progress. Click a player's name for his card, or anywhere else on his row for his points by category. Defensive Contrib. counts the games where a player reached FPL's defensive-action threshold (10 for defenders, 12 for midfielders and forwards), worth 2 points each. Hover any header for the scoring.
